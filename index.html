About This Website

Hello! My name is Khang. This website introduces three places in Vietnam that I would like to explore. Each destination offers a different travel experience.

My Top Three Destinations

  1. Ha Long Bay
  2. Hoi An
  3. Ho Chi Minh City

Places to Explore

Ha Long Bay

Ha Long Bay is known for its limestone islands and beautiful waters. I would love to take a boat trip and enjoy the scenery.

Hoi An

Hoi An attracts me with its historic architecture and colorful lanterns. I would like to walk through the old town and try local food.

Ho Chi Minh City

Ho Chi Minh City is a lively destination with busy streets, modern buildings, and many places to eat. I would like to explore its museums and cafes.

My Travel Checklist

  • Check the weather before leaving.
  • Bring comfortable walking shoes.
  • Carry a reusable water bottle.
  • Respect local customs and keep public spaces clean.
